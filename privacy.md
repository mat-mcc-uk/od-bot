# Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026.

This policy explains what the Happy Jump Insurance bot ("the bot") collects and how it uses it. The bot is run by Ripcorder [4252513] in Torn City.

## What we collect

When you register and use the service, the bot stores:

- Your Discord user ID.
- Your Torn ID and Torn name.
- Your quotes, cover periods, claims and credit balances.
- Giveaway entries and results.
- Records of Xanax sends to our Torn account that were matched to you: the sender's Torn ID, items sent, message, time and the matching quote.
- Your Torn overdose count, read while you have live cover, and the dates it changed.

The bot also lists the members of the Discord server so it can send a welcome message to new members. It keeps the Discord user IDs of members it has already seen so that it does not welcome the same person twice. It keeps a list of which members currently hold the Insured role.

The bot does not read your Discord messages, and it does not collect your email address, IP address or real name. It does not ask for your Torn API key. The only Torn API key it uses is the owner's, which it uses to read the owner's own Torn log and public player data.

## Where the data comes from

- You, when you run commands and press buttons in Discord.
- Discord, which supplies your user ID and server membership.
- The Torn API, which supplies your public profile (name and linked Discord account), the owner's item log and the overdose count in your personal stats.

## How we use it

We use the data to check your registration, issue quotes, match payments, start and end cover, spot possible overdoses, review and pay claims, run giveaways and credits, send you notices and welcome messages, and keep statistics on how the service performs. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Messages from the bot

The bot sends you cover notices by Discord direct message. If your direct messages are closed, it posts the notice in a server channel and mentions you. Staff see claim details, including your Discord mention, Torn name and ID, in a staff-only channel.

## Who has access

The bot's data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider. Staff of the service can see claim and registration details through the bot. Discord and Torn City process data on their own systems under their own policies, and we do not control those.

## How long we keep it

We keep your records while the service runs, because payment and claim history is needed to settle disputes and measure results. You can ask us to delete your registration and personal records at any time, and we will do so unless we need to keep a record of a payment or claim that is still open.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data. Contact Ripcorder [4252513] in Torn City, or staff in the Discord server. You can also stop the bot's direct messages by closing your direct messages from the server, and you can leave the server at any time.

## Children

The service is not meant for anyone under the age Discord and Torn City require to hold an account.

We may change this policy. The current version is always at this address, with its effective date at the top.
